Full Rules and T&C

Full Rules

Everything participants need to know before joining Silver Sparks Season 1.

  1. Join with your wallet: Connect a wallet to enter the campaign. No KYC is required to join, earn Sparks, appear on the leaderboard, or enter the raffle.
  2. What counts as activity: A wallet is active when it completes at least one qualifying interaction inside the STT ecosystem. For Season 1, qualifying interactions are STT Earn and Silver Forecast.
  3. Earn Silver Sparks: Complete campaign actions such as Daily Forecasts, weekly missions, STT holding, STT staking, and eligible referrals.
  4. Sparks are campaign points only: Sparks are non-transferable and do not have a fixed cash value. Rewards are paid only from the official Season 1 reward pools.
  5. Leaderboard is open, rewards are gated: All active wallets can appear on the leaderboard. However, only wallets that hold or stake at least 0.1 STT before the final campaign snapshot can receive rewards from the Leaderboard Pool.
  6. Raffle tickets are automatic: Each participating wallet receives 1 raffle ticket automatically. Every 100 Sparks = 1 additional raffle ticket.
  7. Free users can win raffle rewards: Users below the 0.1 STT holding or staking threshold can still join, earn Sparks, appear on the leaderboard, and enter the raffle. They are eligible for the Raffle Pool only.
  8. Holder and staker tiers: Higher STT holding or staking levels unlock better campaign multipliers. Tier eligibility requires the required STT balance or staked amount to be maintained for the required period.
  9. Multiplier cap: Final Sparks are calculated using campaign multipliers, but the total multiplier is capped at 2.4x.
  10. Fair play rule: Bots, duplicate-wallet farming, fake referrals, coordinated abuse, or abnormal activity may lead to disqualification. The team may review suspicious wallets before rewards are finalized.
  11. Final snapshot and rewards: At the end of the campaign, the leaderboard will be frozen, raffle tickets finalized, winners reviewed, and rewards distributed according to the official campaign results.
  12. Product rules remain separate: Campaign rules are separate from STT minting, redemption, custody, and other product terms. STT product activity remains subject to applicable SilverTimes platform rules.

Season 1 rewards are limited to the fixed campaign pool. Silver Sparks do not represent STT, silver, cash, or any guaranteed redemption value.
